## 1. Getting Started with Buffr

### 1.1 What is Buffr?
Buffr is a digital wallet and payment platform designed for Namibian government grant beneficiaries. It allows you to:
- Receive your grants (vouchers) securely.
- Store money in one or more wallets.
- Send money to family and friends.
- Pay bills (electricity, water, TV, etc.).
- Cash out your money at agents, shops, ATMs, and NamPost.
- Apply for small loans based on your grant history.
- Save towards goals and get financial advice.

Buffr is licensed by the Bank of Namibia and your money is always safe.

### 1.2 How do I create an account?
1. Download the Buffr app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. Open the app and tap **Get Started**.
3. Choose whether you want to receive your one‑time code (OTP) by **SMS** or **email**.
4. Enter your mobile number (starting with +264) or your email address.
5. You will receive a 6‑digit code. Enter it in the app.
6. Provide your first name and last name.
7. Optionally, take a photo or upload a profile picture.
8. Set up Face ID / fingerprint for quick access (you can skip this).
9. Your account is created! You will see your Buffr ID and a virtual Buffr Card.

### 1.3 I lost my phone / changed my number – what do I do?
If you lose your phone or change your number, you need to secure your account as soon as possible:

- **If you still have access to the app:** Go to Profile → Settings → Security and change your PIN. Also, if you changed your number, contact support to update your registered phone number.
- **If you cannot access the app:** Call Buffr Support immediately (see §17) or email **george@buffr.ai**. We will verify your identity and help you regain access.

### 1.4 Is Buffr safe?
Yes. Buffr uses bank‑level security:
- All data is encrypted.
- Your money is safeguarded and kept separate from Buffr's own funds.
- Every financial action requires two‑factor authentication (2FA) – either your PIN plus a biometric or a one‑time code.
- We are regulated by the Bank of Namibia and comply with all national laws.

### 1.5 What if I don't have a smartphone? (USSD)
If you use a **feature phone** (no smartphone or no data), you can still access Buffr services by dialling **\*123#**. The USSD menu lets you:
- Check your **balance**
- **Redeem a voucher** (you will receive an SMS with a cash-out code to use at an agent or NamPost)
- Get a **cash-out code** (to withdraw from your wallet at an agent)
- **Pay bills** (select biller and amount)
- Top up **airtime**
- Request **proof-of-life** or schedule a visit

Every action is confirmed by **SMS**, so you have a record. Critical notifications (e.g. voucher issued, redemption confirmation) are also sent by SMS so you are never left behind. Buffr's mobile app and USSD offer the same core outcomes; use whichever you have access to.

## 2. Wallets and Balances

### 2.1 What is a wallet?
A wallet is like a digital purse inside the app. You can have multiple wallets for different purposes – for example, one for daily spending, one for school fees, one for emergencies. Each wallet has its own balance.

### 2.2 How many wallets can I have?
You can create as many wallets as you like. The first wallet (called "Main") is created automatically. To add more:
- Go to the Home screen and scroll to the **Wallets** section.
- Tap the **+** button.
- Choose an icon (emoji) for your wallet.
- Give it a name (e.g., "School Fees").
- Optionally set up Auto‑Pay (see §2.5).
- Tap **Create**.

### 2.3 How do I add money to my wallet?
You can add money to any of your wallets from the Home screen or from the wallet's detail page:
- Tap **+ Add** on the main balance pill, or go into a wallet and tap **Add Money**.
- Choose a method:
  - **Bank Transfer** – Transfer from your linked bank account (if you have one). You will see the Buffr bank details.
  - **Debit/Credit Card** – Add money instantly using a card. You may need to add your card first (see §9.2).
  - **Redeem Voucher** – Use a government grant voucher to top up (see §3).

### 2.4 How do I transfer money between my own wallets?
1. Go to the wallet you want to move money *from*.
2. Tap **Transfer** (or look for a transfer option in the wallet actions).
3. Select the destination wallet.
4. Enter the amount and any note.
5. Confirm with your PIN or biometric. (Transfers between your own wallets require approval, but no 2FA token is needed.)

### 2.5 What is Auto‑Pay?
Auto‑Pay lets you set up regular automatic transfers from your wallet to a bill, a savings goal, or another wallet. For example, you can set N$200 to move to your "School Fees" wallet every month.

To set it up:
- When creating a wallet, toggle **Auto Pay** on.
- Or go to an existing wallet, tap **Auto Pay**.
- Choose frequency: Weekly, Bi‑weekly, or Monthly.
- Set the day and time.
- Enter the amount and number of repayments (if any).
- Choose the payment method (usually the wallet itself).
- Save.

## 3. Vouchers (Government Grants)

### 3.1 What types of vouchers are there?
Vouchers are electronic grants from the Namibian government. Common types include:
- **Old‑age pension**
- **Disability grant**
- **Child support grant**
- **Veterans' grant**
- **Foster care grant**

The grant type and amount are set by the government and appear in the voucher details.

### 3.2 How do I receive a voucher?
Vouchers are issued by the government on scheduled dates (usually the first of the month). When a voucher is issued to you, you will receive an SMS and an in‑app notification. The voucher will appear in your **Vouchers** tab.

### 3.3 How do I redeem a voucher?
You have three ways to redeem a voucher:

**A. Redeem to your Buffr Wallet (instant, free)**
1. Go to the **Vouchers** tab.
2. Tap the voucher you want to redeem.
3. Tap **Redeem to Buffr Wallet**.
4. Confirm with your PIN or biometric.
5. The voucher amount is added to your chosen wallet instantly.

**B. Cash at NamPost (free)**
1. Follow steps 1‑2 above.
2. Tap **Cash at NamPost**.
3. Choose a branch from the list (you can search by location).
4. Review the branch details and tap **Confirm**.
5. The app will display a QR code and a manual 6‑digit code. This QR is valid for **2 hours**.
6. Go to the NamPost branch with your ID. Show the QR code (or manual code) to the teller.
7. The teller will scan the code; you may be asked to enter your PIN or use biometric on their device.
8. Receive your cash.

**C. Cash at SmartPay Mobile Unit (free)**
1. Follow steps 1‑2 above.
2. Tap **Cash at SmartPay Unit**.
3. Choose a unit from the list (they travel to different locations).
4. Confirm and you will get a QR code (valid for 2 hours).
5. Visit the unit, show your ID and the QR code.
6. The agent will process the cash‑out.

### 3.4 What happens if a voucher expires?
Vouchers have an expiry date clearly shown in the app. You will receive reminders **14 days, 7 days, and 1 day before expiry**. If a voucher expires, you **cannot** redeem it. The government may reissue it, but you must contact the relevant ministry.

### 3.5 Can I share a voucher with someone else?
No. Vouchers are personal and cannot be transferred to another person. Only the named beneficiary can redeem them.

## 4. Cash‑Out Methods

### 4.1 Overview of cash‑out methods
After your money is in a wallet, you can turn it into cash using any of these methods. Each has a different fee and speed.

| Method | How it works | Fee | Time |
|--------|--------------|-----|------|
| **Cash at Till** | Scan a till's QR at a participating retailer (e.g., Shoprite) | Free | Instant |
| **Cash at Agent** | Visit a Buffr agent, scan their QR | N$5 | Instant |
| **Cash at Merchant** | Scan a merchant's QR (e.g., at a shop) | N$3 | Instant |
| **Cash at ATM** | Enter amount on an ATM, scan its QR | N$8 | Instant |
| **Bank Transfer** | Transfer to your linked bank account | N$5 | 1‑2 days |

### 4.2 Cash at Till (Retailer)
1. Go to the wallet you want to cash out from.
2. Tap **Cash Out** and select **Cash at Till**.
3. The app will tell you to scan the till's QR code. (The till will display a QR when you ask for cash‑out.)
4. Scan the QR with your phone.
5. The app shows the retailer name and amount. Enter the amount you want (up to N$5,000 per transaction).
6. Confirm with your PIN or biometric.
7. The retailer hands you the cash immediately.

### 4.3 Cash at Agent
1. Go to the wallet and tap **Cash Out** → **Cash at Agent**.
2. You will see a map/list of nearby agents. Choose one.
3. At the agent, they will show you a QR code. Scan it.
4. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN/biometric.
5. The agent gives you cash (minus the N$5 fee, which is deducted from your wallet).

### 4.4 Cash at Merchant
Similar to Cash at Till, but at participating merchants (shops that are not specifically cash‑out till points). The fee is N$3.

### 4.5 Cash at ATM
1. Go to the ATM and select "Cardless cash" or "QR cash" on the screen.
2. The ATM will display a QR code. Scan it with the app.
3. Enter the amount on your phone and confirm with PIN/biometric.
4. The ATM dispenses the cash. The N$8 fee is deducted from your wallet.

### 4.6 Bank Transfer
1. You must first have a linked bank account (see §9.2).
2. Go to wallet → **Cash Out** → **Bank Transfer**.
3. Select the bank account.
4. Enter the amount and confirm.
5. The money will appear in your bank account within 1‑2 business days. The N$5 fee is deducted from your wallet.

## 5. Sending and Receiving Money

### 5.1 How do I send money to another Buffr user?
1. From the Home screen, tap **Send** (the FAB button) or go to **Send Money** in the quick actions.
2. Choose the recipient from your contacts, or enter their phone number.
3. Enter the amount and an optional note.
4. Select which wallet to send from.
5. Tap **Send** and confirm with your PIN/biometric.
6. The money is sent instantly. Both you and the recipient get a notification.

### 5.2 How do I receive money?
When someone sends you money, you will receive a notification. Tap it to see the details. You can choose to:
- **Add to wallet** – the money is credited immediately.
- **Cash out now** – go directly to the cash‑out screen.

You can also receive money by sharing your QR code. Tap **My QR Code** in the profile or quick actions, and let the sender scan it.

### 5.3 What is a payment request?
If someone (like a merchant or a friend) asks you to pay them, you will get a **payment request** notification. It shows who is requesting, how much, and any note. You can:
- **Pay now** – you are taken to the send money screen with the details pre‑filled.
- **Decline** – the request is dismissed.

## 6. Groups

### 6.1 What are groups?
Groups let you manage shared finances with family, friends, or a savings club. Members can:
- See the group balance and each member's contribution.
- Send money to the group.
- Request money from the group (e.g., for a shared expense).
- Track who has paid.

### 6.2 How do I create a group?
1. Go to **Groups** from the Home screen.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Give the group a name and optional description.
4. Add members by searching your contacts or entering their phone numbers.
5. Tap **Create**.

### 6.3 How do I add or remove members?
- **As a group admin**, open the group, tap the settings icon, and select **Add Members** or **Remove Members**.
- **As a member**, you can only leave the group (remove yourself).

### 6.4 How do I send money to a group?
1. Open the group.
2. Tap **Send** at the bottom.
3. Enter the amount and an optional note.
4. Confirm with PIN/biometric.
5. The money is added to the group's pool. All members see the transaction.

### 6.5 How do I request money from a group?
1. Open the group.
2. Tap **Request**.
3. Enter the amount and a reason (e.g., "For our party").
4. Confirm. Each member will get a request notification.
5. As members pay, the request shows progress (e.g., "3 of 4 paid").

## 7. Loans (Voucher‑Backed Advances)

### 7.1 What is a voucher‑backed loan?
It's a small loan that is automatically repaid from your next government voucher. For example, if you need cash before your next grant, you can borrow a portion of it now.

### 7.2 Who can apply?
Any Buffr user who has received at least one government voucher in the past. Your eligibility is based on your grant history.

### 7.3 How much can I borrow?
The maximum loan is **one third (1/3) of your last voucher amount**. For instance, if your last child grant was N$300, you can borrow up to N$100.

### 7.4 How is the loan repaid?
Repayment is **automatic**. When you redeem your **next** voucher to your wallet, Buffr deducts the loan amount plus 15% interest before crediting the rest. For example, if you borrowed N$100, you will repay N$115. If your next voucher is N$300, your wallet will receive N$185.

### 7.5 What happens if I miss a repayment?
You cannot "miss" it – repayment is automatic as soon as you redeem a voucher. If you don't redeem any voucher, the loan remains outstanding and you cannot apply for another until it's repaid.

## 8. Bill Payments

### 8.1 What bills can I pay with Buffr?
You can pay:
- Electricity (NamPower, City of Windhoek)
- Water
- Internet and TV (e.g., DStv, MTC)
- Municipal rates
- School fees (participating schools)
- Airtime top‑up (MTC, TN Mobile, etc.)

### 8.2 How do I pay a bill?
1. From the Home screen, tap **Bill Payments**.
2. Choose a category (e.g., Electricity).
3. Select the biller (e.g., NamPower).
4. Enter your account number (the one on your bill).
5. Enter the amount and confirm.
6. Approve with your PIN/biometric.
7. You will see a confirmation screen with a reference number. Keep this for your records.

### 8.3 What if my payment fails?
If a payment fails (e.g., due to network issues or incorrect account number), your money is **not deducted**. You will see an error message. Check your account number and try again. If the problem persists, contact support.

## 9. Cards

### 9.1 What is the Buffr Card?
The Buffr Card is a virtual card that represents your main wallet. It shows your name and card number, but it is **not** a physical card you can use at ATMs or shops – it is only for display and identification. To spend your money, use the app's send, cash‑out, or pay features.

### 9.2 How do I add a bank card to Buffr?
You can add a debit or credit card to use for topping up your wallet:
1. Go to **Cards** from the profile or quick actions.
2. Tap **Add Card**.
3. Choose **Scan your card** (point your camera at the card) or **Enter details manually**.
4. Enter the card number, expiry date, CVV, and cardholder name.
5. Tap **Save**.
6. The card will appear in your linked cards. You can now use it when adding money.

### 9.3 Is it safe to store my card details?
Yes. Card details are stored securely and encrypted. Buffr never stores your full CVV after the initial addition. We are certified for card data security (PCI‑DSS compliant).

## 10. Profile and Settings

### 10.1 How do I update my personal information?
- Go to **Profile** (bottom tab) → tap your name/avatar at the top.
- You can change your first name, last name, and profile picture.
- Some fields (like phone number) may require contacting support for security reasons.

### 10.2 How do I change my PIN?
1. Go to **Profile** → **Settings** → **Security**.
2. Tap **Change PIN**.
3. Enter your current PIN, then your new PIN twice.
4. Confirm.

### 10.3 How do I enable Face ID / fingerprint?
1. Go to **Profile** → **Settings** → **Security**.
2. Tap **Biometric login**.
3. Follow your device's instructions to set it up.
4. Once enabled, you can use your face or fingerprint to log in and approve payments.

### 10.4 What is proof‑of‑life?
Proof‑of‑life is a government requirement to confirm that grant recipients are still alive. Every 90 days, you must verify. You will receive reminders.
- **In the app:** Go to **Proof of Life** from the profile or the banner on Home, and follow the biometric prompt.
- **At an agent/NamPost:** Visit any Buffr agent or NamPost branch with your ID; they will verify you using their terminal.
If you don't verify in time, your wallet may be frozen (see §16.5).

## 12. Complaints and Disputes

### 12.1 How do I make a complaint?
You can lodge a complaint in several ways:
- **In the app:** Go to **Help** → **Contact Us** and describe your issue.
- **Email:** Write to **george@buffr.ai** – this is our dedicated support address.
- **Phone:** Call +264 61 123 456 (during business hours).
- **In person:** Visit our office (address in §17.4).

### 12.2 What happens after I complain?
1. We will **acknowledge** your complaint within 24 hours.
2. We will investigate and give you a **substantive response** within **15 business days**.
3. If we need more time, we will let you know and explain why.

### 12.3 What if I am not satisfied with the response?

## 13. Security and Safety

### 13.1 How does Buffr protect my money?
- Your money is safeguarded under Bank of Namibia rules and kept separate from Buffr's operating funds.

### 13.2 What should I do if I suspect fraud?
- **Immediately** change your PIN (Profile → Settings → Security).
- If you cannot access your account, call support right away.

- We will investigate and, if necessary, freeze your account to prevent further loss.

### 13.3 How to avoid scams
- Never share your PIN, one‑time code, or biometric with anyone. Buffr staff will never ask for them.
- Only scan QR codes from trusted sources: agents, merchants, NamPost, or ATMs you are using.
- Be wary of unsolicited messages claiming you have won money or need to "verify" your account – these are scams.
- Always double‑check the amount and recipient before confirming a payment.

### 13.4 Two‑factor authentication (2FA) explained
2FA means you need **two things** to approve a financial action:
- **Something you know** – your PIN.
- **Something you have** – your phone (for biometric or one‑time code).

Even if someone steals your PIN, they cannot approve a transaction without your phone and biometric.

## 14. Regulatory Information

### 14.1 Buffr's licence and oversight
Buffr is licensed by the Bank of Namibia as a **payment service provider** and **electronic money issuer**. We are subject to regular audits and must comply with all national payment system laws.

### 14.2 Electronic Transactions Act (ETA)
- Your digital records and consents are legally binding.
- Your PIN/biometric is considered your **advanced electronic signature**.
- Buffr keeps full transaction records for at least 5 years, as required by law.

### 14.3 Payment System Management Act and Determinations
- **PSD‑1 (Licensing):** Buffr holds a licence and must meet capital, governance, and reporting requirements.
- **PSD‑3 (E‑money):** Your funds are safeguarded under the law; no interest is paid on wallets.
- **PSD‑12 (Cybersecurity):** We use 2FA, encryption, and have strict incident response plans.

### 14.4 NAMQR – National QR Standard
All QR codes in Buffr follow the Bank of Namibia's NAMQR standard. This ensures:
- Each QR is unique and cannot be reused.
- QR codes from merchants are digitally signed and verified.
- A checksum prevents scanning errors.

### 14.5 Open Banking
When you link a bank account, we use secure OAuth 2.0 protocols. Your bank login details are never shared with Buffr; you authenticate directly with your bank. We only receive the authorisation to access your account information or initiate payments.

### 14.6 Tax information and NamRA
- **Fees:** Any fees you pay through Buffr include VAT where applicable; no extra tax is added at checkout.
- **Grants:** Government grant amounts and voucher redemptions are generally **not taxable** for beneficiaries. You do not need to declare grant income as taxable in normal circumstances.
- **Tax authority:** The **Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA)** is responsible for tax collection and taxpayer education. For questions about income tax, VAT, or other taxes, contact NamRA or a tax advisor.
- **Tax education and filing:** NamRA runs the **Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS)** for online tax registration and filing. You can access guides, filing instructions, and forms at **[itas.namra.org.na](https://itas.namra.org.na)**. ITAS offers 24/7 access, secure filing, and helps with compliance.
- **Tax amnesty:** From time to time NamRA may offer relief programmes (e.g. writing off interest and penalties if you settle outstanding tax). Check NamRA's website or announcements for current programmes and deadlines. Buffr does not give tax advice; for your personal situation always consult NamRA or a qualified tax advisor.

### 14.7 NAMFISA and consumer protection
**NAMFISA** (Namibia Financial Institutions Supervisory Authority) is an independent regulator established to supervise **non-banking** financial institutions and to promote **consumer protection** and a stable, fair financial system. It oversees insurers, pension funds, medical aid funds, and similar institutions under the **Financial Institutions and Markets Act (FIMA)**. NAMFISA has a zero-tolerance approach to non-compliance and mistreatment of customers; consumers are encouraged to report malpractices for swift supervisory action.

**Buffr** is **not** regulated by NAMFISA. We are a payment service provider and e-money issuer regulated by the **Bank of Namibia** under the Payment System Management Act and its determinations. So:
- **Complaints about Buffr** (wallets, vouchers, transfers, fees, etc.) → escalate to the **Bank of Namibia** (see §12.4).
- **Complaints about insurance, pension, or medical aid** → contact **NAMFISA** (see §12.5 and §17.6).

### 14.8 FIMA – Financial Institutions and Markets Act
**FIMA** (Financial Institutions and Markets Act) is Namibian law that governs **non-banking** financial institutions – such as **retirement funds**, **medical aid schemes**, **insurers**, and **collective investment schemes**. NAMFISA supervises these institutions under FIMA. The law is designed to give stronger consumer protection, risk-based supervision, and clearer rules for how these institutions must operate.

**Buffr** does **not** fall under FIMA. We provide **payment and e-money** services, which are regulated by the **Bank of Namibia** under the **Payment System Management Act** and related determinations (PSD-1, PSD-3, PSD-12). If you have questions about your pension, insurance, or medical aid, those providers are supervised by NAMFISA under FIMA; contact NAMFISA for consumer support in that sector.

### 14.9 NamClear and the national payment system
**NamClear** is Namibia's **Automated Clearing House**. It clears and settles payments between banks and other participants so that electronic transfers and card payments move safely across the country. NamClear works with the **Bank of Namibia** (through the Namibia Interbank Settlement System, NISS) and supports services such as **NamPay** (electronic fund transfers, including faster transfers) and **NamSwitch** (card transactions). Buffr and other licensed payment providers operate within this national payment system overseen by the Bank of Namibia. You do not interact with NamClear directly; your bank or Buffr connects to it so your payments are processed correctly.

### 14.10 Instant Payment Project (IPP)
The **Bank of Namibia** is building a national **Instant Payment** system (the Instant Payment Programme, or IPP) to make sending and receiving money faster and more inclusive. The project officially started in 2024 and aims to:
- Allow **any device** to be used – including phones without internet (feature phones) – so vendors, taxi drivers, pensioners, and small businesses can participate.
- Create **one interoperable system** so that banks and licensed payment providers (including e‑money and fintech services) can all connect, giving you more choice and lower-cost options.
- Support the **National Payment System Vision and Strategy** and financial inclusion, especially in rural and informal areas.

The instant payment platform is planned to go live in the coming years. When it is available, regulated providers such as Buffr may connect so you can send and receive money instantly across different banks and apps. For the latest status, check the Bank of Namibia website or announcements.

## 16. Troubleshooting Common Issues

### 16.1 I forgot my PIN
- Go to the login screen and tap **Forgot PIN?**.
- You will receive a one‑time code by SMS/email.
- Enter the code and set a new PIN.
- If you have biometric enabled, you can also use that to log in and change your PIN in settings.

### 16.2 My transaction failed – what now?
- First, check your internet connection.
- Make sure you have sufficient balance (including any fees).
- If you used a QR code, ensure it hasn't expired.
- If the error persists, note the error message (e.g., "Insufficient funds", "Invalid QR") and contact support.

### 16.3 The app says "network error"
This usually means your internet connection is unstable. Try:
- Switching between Wi‑Fi and mobile data.
- Restarting the app.
- If the problem continues, the Buffr servers may be temporarily down. Check our social media for updates.

### 16.4 QR code won't scan
- Make sure your camera is clean.
- Hold the phone steady and at a reasonable distance.
- Ensure the QR code is well‑lit and not damaged.
- If scanning a screen, reduce screen brightness.
- You can always use the **manual code** displayed below the QR code – type it into the teller's terminal.

### 16.5 My wallet is frozen
Your wallet may be frozen because:
- You missed proof‑of‑life (see §10.4). You can still redeem vouchers at NamPost/SmartPay; the biometric check there will also unfreeze your wallet.
- Unusual activity was detected (e.g., multiple failed login attempts). Contact support to verify your identity.
- You have an overdue loan? (Loans don't freeze wallets; they just prevent new loans.)

### 16.6 Voucher says "already redeemed"
This means the voucher has already been used. If you believe this is a mistake, contact support immediately. Do not try to redeem it again.

### 16.7 I received an error code – what does it mean?
Common error codes:
- **E001:** Invalid PIN – try again.
- **E002:** Transaction expired – start over.
- **E003:** Insufficient balance.
- **E004:** Voucher expired.
- **E005:** QR invalid or expired.
- **E006:** Network timeout – check connection.
- **E007:** Account locked – too many failed attempts. Wait a few minutes or contact support.
- **E008:** 2FA required – you need to verify with PIN/biometric.
- **E009:** Daily limit exceeded – you cannot cash out more than N$5,000 per day.
- **E010:** Service unavailable – try again later.
