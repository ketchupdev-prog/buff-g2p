## 1. Licensing & Authorization Requirements (PSD-1)

### 1.1 Entity Classification & Licensing Process

#### Non-Bank Financial Institution Requirements
- [ ] **Entity Structure**: Establish separate legal entity dedicated exclusively to payment services
  - Exception: Waiver available if proves undue burden and no user fund compromise
  - Submit waiver request with justification to Bank of Namibia

- [ ] **Application Process**:
  - [ ] Submit Letter of Intent to Director of National Payment System
  - [ ] Include comprehensive business plan with:
    - [ ] Payment service product name
    - [ ] Nature of business
    - [ ] Scheme features and diagrammatic transaction flows
    - [ ] Security features
    - [ ] Project deployment plan (launch date, location)
    - [ ] Transaction charges and user fees
    - [ ] Target market and penetration strategies
  - [ ] Pay non-refundable application fee (as prescribed in Regulations)
  - [ ] Pay authorization fee upon successful assessment

#### Payment Service Provider Categories
SmartPay may apply under one or more categories:
- [ ] **Payment Instrument Issuer** (vouchers as payment instruments)
- [ ] **Payment Facilitator** (if holding/pooling merchant funds)
- [ ] **Third-Party Payment Service Provider** (if providing technology without holding funds)

**Note:** Only ONE application may be submitted at a time; additional categories require 3-month waiting period after full authorization.

## 2. Electronic Money Issuance Rules (PSD-3)

### 2.1 Applicability Assessment

**Key Question:** Does SmartPay issue electronic money?

**Electronic Money Definition:**
Monetary value that is:
- [ ] Stored electronically/magnetically/digitally
- [ ] Issued on receipt of funds
- [ ] Accepted as payment by persons other than issuer
- [ ] Redeemable on demand for cash in Namibia Dollar

**SmartPay Vouchers Analysis:**
- If vouchers are prepaid, stored value → **YES, likely e-money**
- If vouchers are mere coupons/discounts → **NO, not e-money**

**Decision Point:** Determine if SmartPay vouchers meet e-money definition. If YES, proceed with full PSD-3 compliance.

---

### 2.2 Authorization Process (If Issuing E-Money)

#### Non-Bank Institution Process
- [ ] **Formal Application to Bank**:
  - [ ] Include all documents/data prescribed in PSMA E-money Guidelines
  
- [ ] **Application Fee**:
  - [ ] Pay non-refundable fee (prescribed in PSMA E-money Circular)
  - [ ] Not refunded regardless of approval/rejection
  
- [ ] **Authorization Fee**:
  - [ ] Pay upon approval (prescribed in PSMA E-money Circular)
  
- [ ] **Annual Fee**:
  - [ ] Pay before January 31 each year

#### Compliance Timeframe
- [ ] Demonstrate full compliance within 6 months of PSD-3 publication (November 28, 2019)

---

### 2.3 E-Money Scheme Characteristics

#### Mandatory Requirements
- [ ] **Denomination**:
  - [ ] E-money wallets and transactions in Namibia Dollar only
  
- [ ] **Redemption**:
  - [ ] E-money balances redeemed at par value
  
- [ ] **No Interest**:
  - [ ] No interest paid to customers on e-money wallets
  
- [ ] **Loyalty Programs**:
  - [ ] Loyalty bonuses/reward points cannot be converted to cash or e-money
  
- [ ] **Accounting Treatment**:
  - [ ] Customer funds = "accounts payable" (NOT deposits)
  - [ ] Not considered "deposits" under Banking Institutions Act
  
- [ ] **No Credit/Intermediation**:
  - [ ] Cannot offer credit
  - [ ] Cannot intermediate e-money customer funds
  
- [ ] **Transaction & Balance Limits**:
  - [ ] Subject to limits prescribed by Financial Intelligence Centre

---

### 2.4 Trust Account Requirements

#### Account Setup & Management
- [ ] **Trust Account Opening**:
  - [ ] Open with Namibian licensed banking institution
  - [ ] Dedicated to pooling customer/agent funds exchanged for e-money
  
- [ ] **Fund Segregation**:
  - [ ] Trust funds NOT part of e-money issuer's assets/liabilities
  - [ ] Cannot be used to meet issuer's debt obligations
  - [ ] No commingling with other persons' funds (except e-money users/agents)
  
- [ ] **100% Reserve Requirement**:
  - [ ] Aggregate pooled funds ≥ 100% of outstanding e-money liabilities
  - [ ] Daily reconciliation mandatory
  - [ ] Address deficiencies within 1 business day
  
- [ ] **Permitted Uses of Pooled Funds**:
  - [ ] Customer/agent transactions only
  - [ ] Redemptions
  - [ ] Net reductions in outstanding e-money liabilities
  - [ ] Interest payments (under specific conditions - see below)
  
- [ ] **Annual Compliance Confirmation**:
  - [ ] Within 30 days of next calendar year
  - [ ] Furnish Bank of Namibia with proof of compliance

---

### 2.5 Interest on Pooled Funds

#### Interest Management Rules
- [ ] **Interest Earnings Permitted**: Yes, issuer may earn interest
  
- [ ] **Interest Withdrawal Conditions**:
  - [ ] May only withdraw if remaining pooled funds ≥ 100% of outstanding liabilities
  
- [ ] **Permitted Uses of Interest**:
  - [ ] Pay fees/charges for e-money scheme administration
  - [ ] Pay fees/charges for e-money scheme maintenance
  
- [ ] **Public Interest Requirement**:
  - [ ] Interest must benefit e-money scheme
  - [ ] Ensure fees and charges are in public interest

---

### 2.6 Dormant Wallets & Unclaimed Funds

#### Dormancy Definition
- [ ] **Dormant Threshold**: 6 consecutive months without transactions
  - Transactions by customer OR third party count
  
- [ ] **Pre-Dormancy Notification**:
  - [ ] Notify customer 1 month before 6-month period reached
  - [ ] Explain wallet will become dormant unless activity occurs

#### Dormant Wallet Treatment
- [ ] **No Fees on Dormant Wallets**:
  - [ ] Cannot charge fees or interest
  
- [ ] **No Intermediation**:
  - [ ] Cannot intermediate dormant wallet funds
  - [ ] Cannot treat as income

#### Fund Distribution (After Dormancy)
Follow this hierarchy:

1. [ ] **Customer with Banking Account at Issuer**:
   - Return funds to customer's primary banking account
   
2. [ ] **Known Customer (KYC) Without Banking Account**:
   - Contact customer, return full amount
   - If deceased, funds form part of estate
   
3. [ ] **Unknown Customer, Known Sender**:
   - Return funds to sender
   
4. [ ] **Unknown Customer & Sender**:
   - Deposit into separate bank account
   - Hold for 3 years
   - After 3 years, may use to develop e-money scheme
   - Must report balances to Bank of Namibia before use

#### Reporting Requirements
- [ ] **Wallet Termination**:
  - [ ] Terminate dormant e-money wallet
  
- [ ] **Monthly Report to Bank**:
  - [ ] Number of dormant wallets
  - [ ] Cumulative value on dormant wallets
  - [ ] Number of terminated wallets
  - [ ] Cumulative value deposited in separate account

## 3. Card Transaction Regulations (PSD-4)

### 3.1 Applicability to SmartPay Vouchers

**Key Question:** Are SmartPay vouchers "payment cards"?

**Payment Card Definition:**
Any card authorized by Bank as payment instrument, including:
- Associated/linked payment instruments
- Virtual cards
- Tokens

**Prepaid Card Definition:**
Cards with characteristics including:
- Prepaid and reloadable with money
- Allows cash withdrawals
- Can be acquired by financial institution other than issuer
- Allows purchases at retail POS devices or digital channels
- Linked to electronic money wallets

**Assessment:**
- If SmartPay vouchers are card-based → **Full PSD-4 compliance required**
- If SmartPay vouchers are specific-purpose vouchers in closed loop → **May be exempt**

---

### 3.2 Card Issuance Authorization

#### Authorized Issuers Only
- [ ] **Authorization Requirement**:
  - [ ] Only authorized payment instrument issuers may issue cards
  
- [ ] **Alternative Partnership**:
  - [ ] May issue in conjunction with authorized issuer
  - [ ] Requires written agreement
  - [ ] Agreement subject to Bank approval

---

### 3.3 Domestic Card Transaction Processing

**Note:** Applies if SmartPay uses card-based instruments.

#### Mandatory Domestic Processing
- [ ] **Intrabank Transactions**:
  - [ ] Must be processed through domestic financial institution
  
- [ ] **Domestic Acquiring**:
  - [ ] All domestic card transactions acquired by domestic acquirer
  - [ ] Cross-border acquiring by foreign acquirers PROHIBITED
  
- [ ] **POS & ATM Transactions**:
  - [ ] Domestic transactions at POS/ATMs processed, cleared, settled domestically
  - [ ] Cross-border processing PROHIBITED for POS/ATM transactions

#### Card-Not-Present (CNP) Transactions
- [ ] **International Processing Permitted**:
  - [ ] CNP at POS and digital channels may be processed internationally
  - [ ] Requires Bank approval
  
- [ ] **Approval Requirements** (if seeking CNP international processing):
  - [ ] Justify the request
  - [ ] Prove existing domestic infrastructure unable to provide technical specifications
  - [ ] Provide comprehensive processing submission

## 6. Administrative Penalties Framework (PSD-8)

### 6.1 Purpose & Scope

**Understanding the Framework:**
- Promotes high regulatory standards
- Deters contraventions
- Encourages remedial action
- Reduces need for criminal prosecution

**Applicable to:**
- All authorized participants in NPS
- Delays in compliance with Bank's directives, determinations, guidelines, standards, circulars, specifications, orders, notices

---

### 6.2 Assessment Principles

Bank considers these factors when deciding to impose penalties:

#### Nature & Severity
- [ ] Nature of delay in compliance
- [ ] Likelihood of recurrence
- [ ] Whether intentional, deliberate, or negligent
- [ ] Seriousness/severity of delay
- [ ] Impact on PSP, other PSPs, entire NPS
- [ ] Threat or potential threat to NPS

#### Operational Impact
- [ ] Potential to hinder Bank from ensuring NPS safety, security, efficiency, cost-effectiveness
- [ ] Duration and frequency of delay

#### Conduct After Delay
- [ ] Behavior after identification
- [ ] Degree of cooperation during assessment
- [ ] Whether immediately brought to Bank/other agencies' attention
- [ ] Whether effective remedial action taken

#### Prior Actions & History
- [ ] Whether prior Bank actions (notice, warning, meeting, order) proved ineffective
- [ ] Previous disciplinary records in 5-year period before delay
- [ ] History of previous administrative penalties

#### Economic & Financial Factors
- [ ] Whether derived economic benefit from delay
- [ ] Economic impact on third parties
- [ ] Financial condition of PSP (CAMELS rating)

#### Risk Exposure
- [ ] Risks introduced to PSP's business model
- [ ] Risks to settlement/clearing abilities
- [ ] Risks to risk management systems
- [ ] Risks to NPS

---

### 6.3 Administrative Sanctions Hierarchy

#### Step 1: Warning/Instruction
- [ ] Bank may issue warning not to repeat conduct
- [ ] Bank may issue instruction

#### Step 2: Administrative Penalty
- [ ] Proceed to penalty imposition process if warnings ineffective

---

### 6.4 Administrative Penalty Imposition Process

#### Notice of Delay in Compliance
Bank issues notice containing:
- [ ] Full details of PSP
- [ ] Nature of delay
- [ ] Administrative penalty amount Bank intends to impose
- [ ] Right to make written representation within 14 business days

#### Representation Period
- [ ] **14 Business Days** from notice to show cause why penalty should not be imposed

#### If No Satisfactory Explanation
- [ ] Bank informs PSP to pay within **30 business days** of notice receipt

---

### 6.5 Penalty Calculation

#### Maximum Penalty Structure
- [ ] **Daily Penalties**: Not exceeding N$100,000 per day of non-compliance
- [ ] **Total Cap**: Not exceeding N$1,000,000 per case

#### Calculation Start Date
- [ ] From date delay occurred, OR
- [ ] From date Bank became aware of delay

#### Appeal Process Impact
- [ ] During appeal, Bank reserves decision
- [ ] Bank waives penalty amount calculated during appeal

#### Calculation Methodology
Based on:
1. **Assessment Principles** (Section 7 of PSD-8)
2. **Risk Assessment Matrix** (Schedule 2 of PSD-8)

---

### 6.6 Penalty Assessment Matrices

#### Schedule 1: Assessment Principles Matrix
Scores violations on severity levels (0-5) with weights:

**High Weight Factors (Weight 5)**:
- Intent (non-intentional to deliberate)
- Likelihood of reoccurrence (none to significant)

**Medium Weight Factors (Weight 4)**:
- Hindrance to NPS
- Duration (0 months to ≥2 years)
- Frequency of violations
- Continuation after notification/warning
- Informed Bank of delay
- Cooperation with regulator
- Negligence level

**Lower Weight Factors (Weight 3)**:
- Previous disciplinary records
- Economic benefit derived
- History of previous administrative penalties
- Financial condition (CAMELS rating)

**Mitigating Factors** (reduce penalty):
- Voluntary informing of regulator (Weight 3)
- Voluntary restitution/corrective actions (Weight 4)
- Effective compliance program (Weight 5)

#### Schedule 2: Risk Assessment Matrix
Scores risks on severity levels (0-5) with weights:

**Highest Weight Risks (Weight 5)**:
- Systemic Risk
- Settlement Risk

**High Weight Risks (Weight 4)**:
- Operational Risk
- Reputation Risk

**Medium Weight Risks (Weight 3)**:
- Liquidity Risk
- General Business Risk
- Legal Risk
- Credit Risk

#### Schedule 3: Penalty Amount Determination

| Violation Severity Points | Penalty Range |
|---------------------------|---------------|
| 1-10 | N$0 - N$10,000 |
| 11-20 | N$10,001 - N$20,000 |
| 21-30 | N$20,001 - N$30,000 |
| 31-40 | N$30,001 - N$40,000 |
| 41-50 | N$40,001 - N$50,000 |
| 51-60 | N$50,001 - N$60,000 |
| 61-70 | N$60,001 - N$70,000 |
| 71-80 | N$70,001 - N$80,000 |
| 81-90 | N$80,001 - N$90,000 |
| 91-100 | N$90,001 - N$100,000 |
