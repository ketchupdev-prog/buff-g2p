# Redeeming a voucher at NamPost

You can cash out your government voucher at any NamPost branch **free of charge**. At NamPost we use **biometric verification** to confirm your identity and complete the transaction safely.

## Steps

1. Open the Buffr app and go to **Vouchers**.
2. Select the voucher you want to redeem.
3. Tap **Cash at NamPost**.
4. Choose a branch from the list. You can search by location.
5. Review the branch details and tap **Confirm**.
6. The app will display a QR code. Take your phone to the NamPost counter.
7. The teller will scan the QR code. You may be asked to enter your **PIN** or use **biometric** (fingerprint or face) in the app to confirm.
8. **At the branch:** The teller may ask you to verify your identity using **biometric verification** on the counter terminal (fingerprint or face). This is a one-to-one check against your enrolled details and helps keep your grant secure.
9. Once verified, the teller will hand you the cash.

## Biometric verification at NamPost

- NamPost branches use **biometric verification** at the point of redemption. This can be fingerprint or face scan on the terminal (e.g. FP09 POS).
- This verification also counts as **proof-of-life** if your account required it, so you stay eligible for future grants.
- If your wallet was frozen because proof-of-life was overdue, you can still **redeem your voucher at NamPost** by doing this biometric verification at the branch; it will unfreeze your account for future use.

## Important notes

- The QR code is valid for 2 hours. If you don't arrive in time, generate a new one in the app.
- Bring **ID** (passport, driver's licence, or national ID card) to the branch.
- There is **no fee** for cashing out at NamPost.
- If the terminal asks for biometric (fingerprint or face), follow the teller’s instructions; it only takes a few seconds.

## Troubleshooting

- **QR code does not scan:** Ask the teller to enter the **manual code** shown below the QR on your screen.
- **"Voucher already redeemed":** Contact Buffr Support immediately.
- **Biometric not recognised:** Ensure you are using the same finger or face enrolled at registration; if it still fails, the teller can help you use an alternative method or contact support.
