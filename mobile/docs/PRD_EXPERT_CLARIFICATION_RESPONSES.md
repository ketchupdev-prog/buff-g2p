# Buffr G2P PRD – Expert Clarification Responses

**Prepared for:** SWIFT/EMVCo certified expert review  
**Source:** Buffr G2P App PRD (§1–§21), backend/mobile codebase, and appendices  
**Date:** March 2026  

This document answers the expert’s clarification questions using the PRD and implementation as the single source of truth. Where the PRD is silent or ambiguous, this is stated explicitly and recommendations are noted.

**Scale and scope:** Buffr targets the Namibian market; given population size, we do not anticipate more than c. 2M users, which informs scaling and capacity planning.

**"Buffr position / pushback"** — For each item below, this column states Buffr's position: why the current design is acceptable for G2P scope, what is intentionally out of scope or deferred, and what is a partner/regulator responsibility. Recommendations are framed as roadmap where they are not launch blockers.
